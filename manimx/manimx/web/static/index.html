      Keys and mouse
      
      
      Playing
      
      
      
        
      Space
      Play or pause
      
        
      ← →
      One frame back or on (with Shift: one second)
      
        
      Home End
      To the start or the end
      
        
      Drag on the ruler
      Move the playhead
      
        
      L
      Play in a loop
      
        
      M
      Sound off or on
      
      
      
      
      The timeline
      
      
      
        
      Ctrl + wheel, + −
      Zoom in or out (0: see everything)
      
        
      Drag a clip
      Move it, also up or down onto another track
      
        
      Drag a clip's edge
      Cut its start or end off
      
        
      Drag on empty track space
      Select several clips
      
        
      Alt while dragging
      Don't snap to edges, the playhead and cues
      
        
      Drag a cue
      Change when it happens
      
      
      
      
      Editing
      
      
      
        
      S
      Split the clips under the playhead
      
        
      Delete
      Remove the selected clips
      
        
      Ctrl + D
      Duplicate the selected clips
      
        
      Ctrl + C, X, V
      Copy, cut, and paste at the playhead
      
        
      Ctrl + Z
      Undo (Ctrl + Shift + Z: redo)
      
        
      Escape
      Select nothing
      
      
      
      
      Voice
      
      
      
        
      Drag a script part
      Place it on a script track
      
        
      R or ●
      Record the selected script part (or freely, with none selected); again to stop
      
        
      Escape
      While recording: throw the take away
      
        
      A part's arrow
      Show its words in a row under the track
      
        
      Drag a word there
      Change when it is said (with Shift: its whole sentence)
      
        
      T
      Teleprompter on or off
      
        
      C
      Subtitles on or off
      
        
      Drop sound files
      On an audio track: copied into the project and placed there
